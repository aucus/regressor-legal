FAQ

My progress is gone
Progress is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it, and we keep no copy on a server, so it cannot be restored by us. Before changing devices, use Data — Save in the Codex tab and keep iCloud Backup on.
Does it work offline?
Yes, entirely. The only network use is checking whether a newer version exists.
Are there in-app purchases?
Not at this time. Currency used for pulls is earned through play only.
How do I turn off sound or vibration?
Use the buttons at the top right of the battle screen.
I found a bug
Please email us what happened and a screenshot — it helps a great deal.
